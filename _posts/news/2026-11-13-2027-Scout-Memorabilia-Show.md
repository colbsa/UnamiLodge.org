Save the date! The **2027 Scout Memorabilia Show** will take place **Friday, August 27 and Saturday, August 28, 2027** at the **Community Center, Central Schwenkfelder Church**, 2067 Steelman Road, Lansdale, PA 19446.

Join collectors, Scouts, and Scouters to buy, sell, trade, and browse Scout memorabilia and patches. All proceeds benefit the youth of Unami Lodge, One.

Visit [unamilodge.org/show](/show/) for show information and upcoming registration details.
