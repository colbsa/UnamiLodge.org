Brothers,

At Fall Fellowship, the general members of Unami Lodge, One approved two changes to our Rules and Procedures, following approval by the Lodge Executive Committee on September 22:

- **Trading Post Committee** - a new committee to create and sell lodge properties, supporting the Treasurer. The Treasurer's duties and a new Trading Post Chair position are added to the Rules and Procedures.
- **National Events Committee** - the NOAC committee becomes a standing, year-round committee that plans for NOAC and other national events, with a new National Events Chair position.

Under the Rules and Procedures, new operating committees take effect at the start of the following lodge year, so both committees begin on January 1, 2027. The Lodge Executive Committee organization chart (Appendix A) has been updated to include them.

The updated Rules and Procedures, approved by the general members on October 4, 2026, are available below and on the [Rules & Procedures](/rules/) page. If you have suggestions for future changes, please contact the Lodge Operations Committee at lodgeoperations@unamilodge.org.

Yours in Brotherhood,  
The 2026 Lodge Officers
