During Fall Fellowship at Resica Falls Scout Reservation we will be holding our last general members meeting of the lodge year. During this time, all members in good standing are invited to attend and contribute to the future of our Lodge. We will have two business items for all youth and adult program participants to vote upon and contribute to these proposed changes to our Lodge's Rules and Procedures. Pdfs to review the proposed changes are included at the bottom of this article.

We ask that after breakfast on Sunday morning, all members remain in the Dining Hall to attend.

We are so excited to finish off an amazing lodge year with all of you, and hope to see you soon!

Yours in Brotherhood,  
The 2026 Lodge Officers
